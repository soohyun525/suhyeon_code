# Saju — 30-Day Viral Strategy (Goal: 100K users)

## 0. The honest math first

100K users in 30 days ≈ **3,300 new users/day average**, but virality is
exponential, not linear. A realistic winning shape:

| Week | Users (cum.) | Engine |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2–5K | Seeding: creators, communities, friends-of-friends |
| 2 | 10–20K | First viral format lands; share loop compounds |
| 3 | 35–60K | Creator wave 2 + trend momentum |
| 4 | 80–120K | Peak virality + press/aggregator pickup |

This only works if the **K-factor** (invites generated per user × conversion)
stays **≥ 0.35** and the **viral cycle time** stays **< 24h**. Everything below
is engineered around those two numbers. Pure organic can do this (astrology-tok
does it monthly), but a small creator-seeding budget ($2–5K) roughly doubles the
odds. Plan for both.

## 1. Positioning — one sentence

> **"MBTI walked so Saju could run."**
> The 1,000-year-old Korean personality read — your K-drama-coded cosmic type,
> from your exact birth minute.

Why this wins now:
- **Astrology-tok** is a perpetual-motion machine (#astrology 100B+ views) but
  Western zodiac content is saturated — same 12 signs since 2019.
- **K-everything** (K-pop, K-drama, K-beauty) is the strongest culture carrier
  to Gen-Z globally. Saju is the *untapped* K-vertical: familiar shape
  (birth chart), fresh lore (Four Pillars, 십성, 귀인).
- **Identity content is the most shared content.** People don't share apps;
  they share *themselves*. That's why the product's core viral unit is a
  **type**, not a reading.

## 2. Product loops (built ✅ / next ⏭)

### ✅ Built this sprint
1. **Saju Type archetypes** — 10 meme-able identities ("Main Character Sun ☀️",
   "Moonlight Mist 🌙"). The MBTI-style hook: instantly comparable, arguable,
   taggable. Shown as the hero of every result.
2. **Photocard generator** — one tap renders a 1080×1350 K-pop-photocard-style
   image (type, hanja pillars, share line) → native share sheet on mobile
   (straight into IG stories/TikTok), download on desktop. *The screenshot IS
   the ad.*
3. **AI share line** — every free reading includes one screenshot-worthy
   sentence written for that person's chart, plus a **copy-caption** button
   (caption ends with "what's your saju type? ✨" — a built-in CTA).
4. **OG tags** tuned so every pasted link asks the reader a question
   ("What's your K-Destiny type?").

### ⏭ Next (in priority order — each adds a loop)
1. **Friend compatibility (궁합) link** — "compare our charts" generates a
   shared result page. Two-player features are the strongest K-factor lever
   (each use *requires* an invite). Target: week 1–2.
2. **Daily fortune (오늘의 운세)** — 15-second daily return loop; enables push
   /email re-engagement and "today's energy" story templates. Target: week 2.
3. **Type leaderboard / "rarest types"** — social proof + FOMO ("only 6% of
   users are Gemstone 💎"). Cheap to build once traffic flows.
4. **Idol-energy angle (safe version)** — never claim real idols' birth data;
   instead "your type shares energy with the *4th-gen main vocal* archetype".
   Users will do the idol-matching themselves in comments — that's the point.

## 3. Channel plan (owned)

**TikTok (primary), IG Reels (repost), Shorts (repost).** 2 posts/day, 30 days.
Formats that already work in this niche (remix, don't invent):

| Format | Example hook |
|---|---|
| Type reveal POV | "POV: you find out you're a Steel Idol ⚔️ and suddenly your whole life makes sense" |
| Type-sorting slideshow | "Which Saju type is the most chaotic? ranking all 10" |
| Duet-bait | "Show me your saju type without telling me your saju type" |
| Reading-reaction | Screen-record a real (consented) reading, react live |
| K-culture explainer | "Why every Korean parent checks 궁합 before you date" |
| Call-out | "If you're a Wildflower 🌷 you NEED to hear this" |

Rules: type names always on screen in the first 1.5s; every video ends on the
question CTA; pin a comment with the link; reply to top comments with
mini-readings (comment-section virality is half the growth).

**X/Threads:** type-list threads ("the 10 saju types as red flags"), quote-RT
bait ("drop your birth time, I'll tell you your type" — manual readings for the
first 48h; unbeatable cold-start engagement).

**Reddit (week 1, careful):** r/kpopthoughts, r/astrology, r/AsianBeauty
adjacent — post as *story/explainer*, not ad ("I built a free tool that does
Korean four-pillars readings in English — here's what it got scarily right").

## 4. Creator seeding (the accelerant)

- **Targets:** 50 micro-creators (10–100K followers) across astrology-tok,
  k-pop-tok, and "that girl"/self-care niches. Micro > macro: better CPM,
  more authentic, duet-able.
- **Offer:** free premium code + personalized reading of *their* chart
  (creators post content about themselves; give them themselves as content).
  Pay only proven performers ($50–200/post) in wave 2.
- **The ask:** post their photocard + reaction, duet-bait their audience
  ("what type are you? link in bio").
- **Korean-diaspora creators first** — cultural authority ("my grandma
  literally does this") converts skeptics.

## 5. Launch moments

- **Day 1–2:** X manual-reading thread + 3 subreddit story posts + first 10
  creator posts staggered across 48h (algorithm reads clustered engagement as
  a trend).
- **Week 2:** Product Hunt launch (astrology/AI crossover does well) +
  "10 types" explainers wave.
- **Week 3–4:** ride whatever format won — double down 10:1 on the winner.
  Tie posts to the K-calendar (comebacks, drama finales: "every member of
  [group] probably has this energy" — archetype language, no real birth data).

## 6. Conversion & retention tuning

- Free tier stays *genuinely good* (type + full chart + personality + inner
  conflict) — generosity is the growth engine; the paywall sells **depth**
  (2026 forecast, 궁합, 귀인), not the identity.
- **First-session offer:** $9 → $5 for 24h after first reading (urgency
  without being gross). A/B once traffic > 1K/day.
- Post-payment share prompt: "gift 3 friends a free premium section" (turns
  buyers into inviters).
- Daily fortune (once built) is the retention spine: D1 target ≥ 25%.

## 7. Metrics that decide everything

Track from day 1 (add a lightweight analytics event on: reading created,
photocard saved, caption copied, checkout started, paid):

| Metric | Target | If below target |
|---|---|---|
| K-factor | ≥ 0.35 | Strengthen share loop (photocard prompt timing, compatibility feature) |
| Photocard save rate | ≥ 20% of readings | Move share CTA higher; auto-preview the card |
| Viral cycle time | < 24h | More question-CTAs; comment-reply readings |
| D1 retention | ≥ 25% | Ship daily fortune sooner |
| Reading completion | ≥ 70% | Shorten free reading top; move shareLine up |
| Free→paid | 2–4% | Tune paywall copy/price, not the free tier |

**Weekly kill/scale rule:** every content format gets 6 posts; below-median
formats die, the best format gets 10× production. No sentimentality.

## 8. Risks & honesty

- **100K/30d is a top-decile outcome.** The plan makes it *possible*, not
  promised. The realistic floor with disciplined execution is 15–30K; the
  ceiling (one format truly hits) is 300K+.
- **Platform risk:** TikTok reach is lumpy — that's why X threads + Reddit +
  creators run in parallel.
- **Cultural respect:** Saju is real heritage, not a costume. Keep the
  explainer content reverent ("this is how it actually works") — it's also
  the best-performing content category. Never fabricate idol birth data.
- **Cost watch:** each free reading costs an LLM call. At 100K users, cache
  aggressively (archetype + template hybrid for spikes) or add a soft
  queue. Premium margin covers it from ~2% conversion.
